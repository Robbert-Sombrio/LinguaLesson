# Hegemony: script for Sean (about 36 s)

This word shows up in The Economist all the time. It scares people off.

Hegemony. hi-JEM-uh-nee. Stress on the second syllable.

It looks like a medical term. It means dominance: one company or one country calling the shots.

The Economist writes about "dollar hegemony." Translation: the world runs on dollars.

Try it at work: "Our competitor has hegemony in the budget segment."

You'll sound like you read The Economist over breakfast. Because you did.
