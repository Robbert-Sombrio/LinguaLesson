Hegemony shows up in The Economist all the time, and a lot of smart people skip right past it.

It looks like a medical term. It means dominance: one company or one country calling the shots.

The Economist writes about "dollar hegemony." Translation: the world runs on dollars.

It works in a meeting too: "Our competitor has hegemony in the budget segment." That's a sharper way to say they own the market.

Pronunciation: hi-JEM-uh-nee, with the stress on the second syllable.
