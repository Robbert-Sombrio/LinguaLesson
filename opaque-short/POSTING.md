## LinkedIn (opaque-linkedin.mp4 + opaque-linkedin.srt)

**Post**
Here's a word you'll see in business news: opaque.

Literally, it means you can't see through something, like frosted glass. In business, it means hard to understand.

"The company's finances remain opaque to investors." The numbers exist. Investors just can't see what's behind them.

The opposite is transparent.

It's also a useful word at work. "This process is a little opaque" says something isn't clear, and keeps the focus on the process.

Pronunciation: oh-PAYK, with the stress on the second syllable.
