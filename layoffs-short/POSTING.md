**Post**
Business Insider ran this headline: "Companies laying off staff this year include Meta, Amazon, and Dropbox."

If you've been through a round of cuts, the words you use about it matter.

Laid off: the company cut your job.
Fired: the company cut you.

They're different stories. In an interview or on your profile, say it plainly: "I was laid off in a round of cuts." It's honest, and it puts the decision where it belongs.

One pronunciation detail: the noun is a LAY-off, and the phrasal verb is to lay OFF.
