# Layoffs: script for Sean (about 18 s + the English Edge ending, ~30 s total)

Business Insider ran this headline: "Companies laying off staff this year include Meta, Amazon, and Dropbox."

Laid off and fired mean different things.

Laid off: the company cut your job. Fired: the company cut you.

So in an interview, say: "I was laid off in a round of cuts."

One more thing. The noun is a LAY-off. The phrasal verb is to lay OFF.
