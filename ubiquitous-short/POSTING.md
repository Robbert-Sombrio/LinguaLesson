## LinkedIn (ubiquitous-linkedin.mp4 + ubiquitous-linkedin.srt)

**Post**
A client used this word in a session recently, and I had to stop and explain it: ubiquitous.

It means everywhere. More than common. The kind of everywhere you can't escape.

AI tools are a good example. They're on every desk, in every app, in every meeting. They're ubiquitous.

So the next time someone in a meeting says "these tools are everywhere," you have a sharper option: "They're ubiquitous."

Pronunciation: yoo-BIK-wih-tus, with the stress on the second syllable.
