Working less, getting more: the four-day week
Key vocabulary
attrition
/əˈtrɪʃ.ən/
Staff leaving a company over time.
“Attrition fell by 57% during the trial.”
to pilot
/ˈpaɪ.lət/
To test an idea on a small scale first.
“61 firms piloted the scheme.”
pushback
/ˈpʊʃ.bæk/
Resistance from the people affected.
“Managers expected pushback from clients.”
make the case
/meɪk ðə keɪs/
To argue clearly that something is right.
“The data made the case for staying.”
Comprehension questions
  • 1.Why did attrition fall during the trial?
  • 2.What did firms measure besides revenue?
  • 3.Which roles were hardest to fit into four days?
Grammar focus
Inversion for emphasis
Put the negative adverb first to sound more formal and persuasive in meetings.
“Not only did revenue hold steady, but sick days fell by 65%.”
Discussion
  • 1.Could your team deliver the same output in four days? What would break first?
  • 2.How would you pitch the idea to a sceptical CEO?
