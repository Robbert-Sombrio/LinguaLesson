## LinkedIn (boildown-linkedin.mp4 + boildown-linkedin.srt)

**Post**
Meetings drag when the main point stays buried.

"Boil down to" fixes that. It means: the main point is. Picture a sauce on the stove. The water goes, and only the flavour is left.

The Economist put it well: "Doing business, after all, boils down to managing a complex web of relationships with customers, suppliers and others."

In your next meeting, try: "The decision boils down to cost versus risk." One sentence, and the room knows what to discuss.

It works in negotiations and presentations too. It tells people you've done the thinking.
