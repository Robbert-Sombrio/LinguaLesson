# Boil down to: script for Sean (about 24 s + the English Edge ending, ~35 s total)

Here's a phrase for meetings: boil down to.

It means: the main point is. Like boiling a sauce until only the flavour is left.

The Economist put it this way: "Doing business boils down to managing a complex web of relationships."

In your next meeting, try: "The decision boils down to cost versus risk."

One sentence, and everyone knows what matters.
