# LinguaLesson voiceover script (short version)

**For:** a 3–5 minute narrated version of the walkthrough. Your voice over the screen recordings, no camera.
**Length:** about 620 words, so roughly 4 minutes 15 seconds at a relaxed pace.
**Demo article:** BBC Future, *'Think outside the bots': How to stop AI from turning your brain to mush* (6 May 2026)

How to read this:
- Each line is one breath. Pause briefly at every line break.
- `(beat)` means a full 1-second pause.
- **Bold** marks the word to lean on. There's only 1 or 2 per section.
- `[SCREEN: ...]` is what's on screen. Don't read it out.
- `[SAY: ...]` is the only spot where you fill something in yourself.

## Recording notes

1. **One take per section.** Record each section as its own take. Leave 3 seconds of silence before and after it.
2. **If you stumble,** pause, then restart the whole sentence. Don't patch mid-word. That makes the stumbles easy to cut in Descript.
3. **Room and mic.** Use a small, soft room (a closet full of clothes is perfect), with the mic or phone about a hand's width from your mouth.
4. **Pace.** Read slower than feels natural. Your audience is listening in their second language.
5. **File.** Send WAV if you can. A high-quality M4A or MP3 is fine too.
6. **When you send it,** tell me which vocabulary word you used in section 5, so the screen recording matches.
