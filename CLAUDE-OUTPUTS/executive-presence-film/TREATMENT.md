# Executive presence: 9 words and phrases
Style: **Fluent Edge Swiss** (own style, based on Swiss Motion Graphics `swiss-motion`) · 1920×1080 · 24 fps · ~3:00 · narrator: ElevenLabs "Brian" · English voice and subtitles · light mallet score

## 1. Three candidate structures

1. **The room.** A grid of slate dots is a meeting room. One coral dot is the person with presence. Each term is a chapter that rearranges the room around that dot. Ending: the room empties, the coral dot stays.
2. **The timetable.** The 5 real moments (2007, 2009, 2010, 2014, 2020) laid out along a column grid of years, filled in chronologically. Tidy, but it puts the definitions (gravitas, measured, BLUF) outside the timeline and the order fights the script.
3. **The specimen sheet.** Each term is a module of a 3×3 grid, like a type specimen. Chapters push into one module at a time, and the recap pulls out to the full sheet.

**Chosen: 1 + 3.** The room gives the hook and a protagonist (the coral dot is "presence" itself: the one thing that doesn't obey the grid). The specimen sheet gives the chapter system and the recap. The script already runs in numbered order, which suits the Swiss "programme": one rule applied 9 times.

## 2. Logline and arc
One coral dot walks into a room of grey dots and the whole room leans in. Over 9 chapters we learn the words for what it's doing, and at the end the viewer is told they can be that dot.

- **Setup (0:00–0:15):** the room. The coral dot enters, off-grid. The room re-flows toward it.
- **Development (0:15–2:40):** 9 chapters. Each is a spread: giant numeral on the left page, the term, its meaning and a real-world caption row on the right. The coral dot is the full stop after every term.
- **Turn (2:05–2:25, "Let it land"):** the music stops dead. 2 s of silence. The film itself does what the term says.
- **Ending (2:40–3:00):** pull out to the 3×3 specimen wall of all 9 terms; knife cuts clear it; the coral dot stays in the corner; end card.

## 3. Benchmark
- **Learn from:** Pentagram and Experimental Jetset identity motion (snaps on the beat, flush-left type, huge numerals next to tiny notes); the BBC Ideas / Vox editorial explainers for pacing narration against type (one idea per screen, generous holds).
- **Don't take:** any specific layout, typeface, logo or colour; no Müller-Brockmann compositions.

## 5. Beat sheet
Fixed by the narration: every chapter starts on the word "Number" (whisper word times), quantised to the next eighth note of the score. Text appears with the spoken word, ±1 frame.

## 6. Cue map
- Tempo **96 bpm** (beat 0.625 s, eighth 0.3125 s), 4/4. Bars are the snap grid.
- **Intro (shots 1–2):** single vibraphone pulse on beats 1 and 3.
- **Chapters 1–3:** marimba ostinato in eighths (A minor pentatonic, soft), felt-kick on 1.
- **Chapters 4–7:** add a second marimba line a fifth up and a closed hat on the off-beat (one instrument added per rule).
- **Chapter 8:** band thins to vibraphone only; hard stop of everything on the downbeat before "The silence did the work."; 2 bars of near-silence; the coral dot's felt thump is the first sound back.
- **Chapter 9:** half-time marimba (quarter notes), "measured".
- **Recap + end:** full ostinato returns, then a final vibraphone chord on the end card, ring out.

## 8. Subtitle and title design
- **One family: DM Sans** (brand font, OFL), weights 400/500/700/800. Flush left, ragged right, on a 12-column grid, 96 px margins.
- **Subtitles live in the layout:** the spoken line sits at column 7 of the right page, 34 px slate, each word clip-revealed on its whisper timestamp; held ≥ max(1.8 s, speech + 0.6 s). Also exported as `executive-presence.srt`.
- **Terms:** 120–150 px ink, weight 800, −2 % tracking, followed by the coral dot as a full stop.
- **Numerals:** 520 px, weight 800, sky-blue, optically flush left.
- **Caption rows:** 19 px, weight 500, blue `#577399`, 1 px slate rule above.
