## Title
Ambivalence in 30 seconds: say it and use it

## Description
How do you pronounce "ambivalence," and what does it mean? It's am-BIV-uh-lens, stress on the 2nd syllable, and it means feeling 2 conflicting things at the same time.

Harvard Business Review used it in a headline about AI at work. Employees are excited that AI saves them time, and terrified it'll replace them. That's ambivalence.

Next time a colleague says "I have mixed feelings about this," try: "That's ambivalence. It's normal."
